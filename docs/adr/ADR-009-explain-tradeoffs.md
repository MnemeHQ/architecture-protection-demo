# ADR-009: Explain Trade-offs in ADRs

## Status
Active

## Context
Decisions without recorded rationale become mysterious constraints. Future maintainers need to know *why*, not just *what*.

## Decision
Every ADR includes a "Rationale" or "Context" section explaining the alternatives considered and why the chosen path was taken. Document rejected options and their drawbacks.

## Consequences
- Prevents re-litigation of settled decisions
- Captures institutional knowledge
- Helps evaluate if context has changed

## Enforcement
Guidance only — no mechanical enforcement planned.
